# Civilization Lab v0.7 — Research & Measurement Protocol
Updated: 2026-10-18. Status: research-informed exploratory instrument, NOT a validated assessment, medical tool, causal inference estimator or calibrated prediction model.

## Purpose
Identify observable social/organizational conditions; distinguish claims from supporting evidence; formulate competing causal hypotheses and reversible improvement plans; support later pre/post and comparative evaluation. This instrument must not attribute inherent harmful traits to ethnicity, religion, region, neurotype, family structure or other demographic identities.

2. Safe Work Australia, psychosocial hazards: assess exposure **frequency, duration, severity**, combined hazards and worker consultation. Applies particularly to workplace organizational justice, job demands/control, bullying and poor support. https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/safety-topic/managing-health-and-safety/mental-health/managing-risks/identifying-assessing-controlling-and-reviewing
3. ISO 45003:2021: guidance for psychosocial risk management in occupational health/safety; referenced conceptually, NOT a claim of certification or access to licensed standard text. https://www.iso.org/standard/64283.html
4. AIFS 2026 protective-factor scoping review: responsive parent relationships, parental wellbeing, social support, community links; findings show complexity, variation and knowledge gaps. Do not infer individual child outcomes deterministically. https://aifs.gov.au/all-research/research-reports/rapid-scoping-review-protective-factors-parent-and-child-wellbeing
5. AIFS inter-parental conflict and Australian children: population associations; long-term conflict can matter and some outcomes recover when circumstances change. No personal diagnosis. https://aifs.gov.au/research/commissioned-reports/inter-parental-conflict-parenting-and-child-development-australian
6. WHO/UNICEF/World Bank Nurturing Care: safety/security, responsive caregiving, early learning, good health, adequate nutrition; the v0.7 question coverage is inspired by these dimensions but is not a validated questionnaire. https://nurturing-care.org/nurturing-care-framework-toolkit/
7. OECD 2024 Drivers of Trust: reliability, responsiveness, integrity, openness and fairness. This informs government/community domains and source triangulation. https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-survey-on-drivers-of-trust-in-public-institutions-2024-results_9a20554b-en.html

## Evidence schema — conceptual
- Case / entity: sector, goal, participants and decision rights, geography insofar as services and law differ (never demographic blame).
- Observation: time, observable behaviour or measurable condition, affected population, frequency, duration, **impact**, protective assets.
- Provenance: respondent role (decision-maker, affected person, independent observer, user-reported mixed), documentary/data source claims, disagreements. Every claim from the demo is UNVERIFIED.
- Outcome: baseline before intervention, desired target, observation frequency and data quality; adverse events and unintended effects.
- Hypothesis: directional mechanism, alternative explanations, critical assumptions, disconfirming evidence and uncertainty.
- Intervention: action, owner, scope, feasibility, cost, safety constraints, rollback condition, review date.
- Longitudinal record: timestamped observation, any model version change, outcomes measured and recalibration findings.

## Six sector variants
- Family: physical/emotional safety, caregiver relationship, parental resources and mental wellbeing, nutrition/housing/health, learning and school access, social support, child agency and neurodivergent accommodations. NEVER score neurotype/heritage as pathology or parental fault.
- Company: strategy and customer outcomes, cash flow, roles/decision rights, governance and organizational justice, capability, psychosocial safety, workload, feedback and learning. Allegations about identifiable individuals require procedural fairness and independent checking.
- School: student safety, attendance/participation, access and accommodations, teacher conditions, longitudinal learning progress, student/family voice.
- Community: governance, inclusion/representation, safety and conflict, resource transparency, member support and observed results.
- Nonprofit: mission/results, beneficiary voice, safeguarding, resource reliance, financial quality, evidence of outcome.
- Government: access, reliability, equity, integrity, responsiveness and openness (OECD).

## Assessment policy
1. "Unknown" MUST NOT be interpreted as "No problem". The first output is coverage and missing/uncorroborated perspectives.
2. Answers are separate on occurrence, impact, duration, evidence provenance. Product flags missing source diversity, protective factors and objective longitudinal data.
3. Priority order is an **inspection queue** derived from self-reported observations, not a probability, risk estimate or statement about someone being guilty.
4. Safety indicators override casual experiment suggestions. Users should seek local professional support where appropriate.
5. No exact numeric future score, probability or timeline for an individual is issued until a model is independently trained, validated, calibrated and audited for its specific application.
6. "No change", "one change", "change + support" are qualitative conditional hypotheses. They are NOT identified counterfactual causal estimates.
7. Promote a community idea to trial only after provenance/independent review, consent for affected persons, domain-specific safety check, outcome definitions and rollback plan. Votes demonstrate interest, not factual truth.
8. Current front end is offline, no accounts, no central database, no AI-powered specialist assistant; ideas are stored in browser localStorage. No paid AI enabled.
9. For real multi-party assessment, access and consent must be verified; no public display of personal or health records. Sensitive or identifiable information must not be uploaded without proper legal basis and safeguarding.
10. Domain validity must be established separately: pilot comprehension interviews, inter-rater agreement, reproducibility, construct validity, criterion validity, cultural/language measurement equivalence, prospective calibration, drift monitoring and external replication.

## Evaluation / release gates
- Stage A (current): navigation, scoped sectors, clear question definitions, multi-source evidence claims, qualitative scenarios; automated unit tests and GitHub Pages preview.
- Stage B: branching interviews and surveys, structured case governance, secure permissioned database, authentic multiparty assessments and evidence attachments, moderation.
- Stage C: backtesting using historical data, external validations, expert review and documented uncertainty; do not market predictive accuracy before evidence exists.
- Stage D: consented, reversible randomized/controlled (when possible) interventions, pre-registered metrics and assessment of unintended consequences.

## Open research problems
- Questionnaire coverage across age groups, jurisdictions, sizes and cultures without stereotyping.
- Multi-source missing-not-at-random and selection bias.
- Inference under confounding, spillover effects and feedback loops.
- Long-term generalization and drift.
- Human safety and privacy in sensitive personal scenarios.

No empirical accuracy, clinical validity, independent audit or live multi-user functionality is claimed for v0.7.
